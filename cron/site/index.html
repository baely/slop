Syntax

*
Every value.
*/n
Every nth value, starting at the field minimum.
a-b
The inclusive range a through b.
a-b/n
Every nth value within the range.
a,b,c
A list. Items may themselves be ranges or steps.
JAN-DEC
Month names, in the month field, case-insensitive.
SUN-SAT
Day names, in the day-of-week field. 0 and 7 both mean Sunday.
@daily
Also @hourly, @weekly, @monthly, @yearly, @midnight.

When day-of-month and day-of-week are both restricted, cron fires when either one matches — 0 0 13 * FRI runs on every Friday and on the 13th of every month, not only on Friday the 13th. Fire times are computed in your browser's local timezone by stepping a real calendar forward; nothing is sent anywhere.
