      How it works
      
    
        
      
      • The image is drawn to an offscreen canvas capped at 320px on the long edge with smoothing off, so sampling reads true pixel values and a 40-megapixel scan does not hang the tab.
        • 
      
      • Colours are clustered with median cut implemented from scratch — deterministic, single pass, no seeding or convergence to babysit.
        • 
      
      • Fully transparent pixels are skipped rather than composited to black. Shares are a percentage of the opaque pixels sampled.
