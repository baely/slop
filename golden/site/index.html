Time base

 

 

Sun altitudes are geometric; sunrise and sunset use the −0.833° convention (upper limb, mean refraction). Golden hour is −4° to +6°, blue hour −6° to −4°. Seconds are the model's precision, not the sky's — expect around a minute of disagreement with published tables, more above 60° latitude.
