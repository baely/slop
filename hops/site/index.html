    Notes

    • Missing probes (*) are counted as loss and excluded from best, average and worst — a timeout never drags an average toward zero.
    • tracert reports anything sub-millisecond as <1 ms; those probes are scored as 0.5 ms and shown verbatim.
    • mtr prints 0.0 across the timing columns for a hop at 100% loss. That is absence, not a fast hop, so it reads as no reply.
    • Scope is decided from the address alone: RFC1918, RFC6598 CGNAT, link-local, ULA, loopback, multicast or public. No lookups, no network.
    • Everything runs in the page. The pasted text is kept in localStorage and goes nowhere else.
